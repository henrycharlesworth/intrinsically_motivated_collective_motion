Inner ring: what it sees right now (other birds' silhouettes)
Dots: every collision-free position it could reach within τ steps, coloured by first move; the shown move is bright

Each step, every bird imagines all its move sequences τ steps ahead, assuming the others fly straight on. The outer ring is drawn for one first move (the chosen one unless you click another). Take all the futures that start with that move. For each direction around the bird, p is the share of those futures in which a neighbour fills that direction. The ring's thickness is 4p(1−p): zero where every future agrees (always empty or always blocked), fullest where it's a coin flip. The area of the ring is the move's score W, and the bird picks the move with the most.
