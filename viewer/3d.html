Its whole visual sphere, unrolled: longitude across (ahead in the middle), elevation up and down, in a frame that turns with the bird but stays level with gravity. Each neighbour is a disc; nearer ones are brighter. The thin line is the horizon, the ring marks where the bird is pointing.

Branch score W, by first move

Every step each bird imagines all its move sequences τ steps ahead, assuming the others fly straight on, and discards those that collide. For each first move, W measures how different the views at the end of its surviving futures are from each other, summed over the whole sphere. It picks the move with the largest W.
